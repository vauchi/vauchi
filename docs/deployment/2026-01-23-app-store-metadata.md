## Full Description

### Google Play (4000 chars max)

**Exchange contact cards in person. Update your info anytime.**

Vauchi is a privacy-first contact sharing app. When you meet someone, scan a QR code to exchange contact cards. Update your phone number, email, or address later — everyone who has your card sees the change automatically.

**How it works:**
1. Meet someone in person
2. Scan their QR code (or let them scan yours)
3. Done! You now have each other's contact cards

**Why Vauchi?**

• **Always current** — Change your number? Everyone who has your card sees the update. No need to text everyone individually.

• **Privacy by design** — Your data stays on your device. We can't read your contacts, even if we wanted to. End-to-end encrypted.

• **No accounts** — Your phone IS your identity. No email, no password, no tracking.

• **Control what you share** — Show your work email to colleagues, personal number to friends. Per-contact visibility settings.

• **Works offline** — QR exchange works without internet. Updates sync when you're back online.

• **Recover your contacts** — Lost your phone? Friends can vouch for you to restore access.

**Security:**
• End-to-end encryption (X3DH + Double Ratchet, same as Signal)
• Zero-knowledge relay (server sees only encrypted blobs)
• No tracking, no analytics, no ads
• Open source (github.com/vauchi)

**Permissions:**
• Camera: Required for QR code scanning
• Internet: Required for syncing updates
• No access to your existing contacts

Vauchi is free, open source, and community-funded. No VC money, no data harvesting.
